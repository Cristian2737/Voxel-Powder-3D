VOXEL POWDER 3D — DROP-IN UPDATE v0.4
=====================================

Filename stays Voxel_Powder_3D_Update.zip for every update.

1. Extract this ZIP into your existing Voxel Powder 3D folder.
2. Replace existing files when Windows asks.
3. Run RUN_STANDALONE.bat (Java 8 or newer).

NEW — ELECTRONICS
- Press E to toggle standard / electronics hotbars.
- Press number keys 1–9 to select tools/materials.
- SPRK is a non-destructive tool: right-click the targeted conductor.
- BTRY automatically pulses neighboring conductors.
- PSCN enables SWCH and carries power toward NSCN; NSCN turns switches off.
- INST carries a charge instantly across a connected 3D line.
- INWR limits conductive connections.
- LCRY illuminates when charged.
- The demo world's north side has two electronic circuit examples.
- P pauses the simulation if you need to build a circuit without it running.

Forge is source-only until compilation in a Forge development environment.
The full ZIP includes Forge electronics blocks and a separate SPRK injector item.
